# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the people involved and the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Admin**:
Someone who runs Cadence itself. An Admin can act on any course.
_Avoid_: Admin (for a Team Manager)

**Instructor**:
Someone who creates and teaches courses. Every course has exactly one Instructor, and an Instructor can only act on their own courses.
_Avoid_: Teacher, Author, Owner

**Learner**:
Someone enrolled in a particular course, whether they bought it themselves or claimed a seat from a team licence. Being a Learner is about a course, not an account: an Admin or Instructor who enrolls in a course is a Learner on it. A learner whose Refund Request is approved is no longer a Learner on that course.
_Avoid_: Student (for someone enrolled in a course), Customer

**Student**:
The kind of account for anyone who isn't an Admin or an Instructor. It says nothing about which courses they're enrolled in.
_Avoid_: Learner (for the kind of account)

**Team Manager**:
The person who buys a team licence and hands its seats out to Learners. A Team Manager has no say over Refund Requests other than their own.
_Avoid_: Admin, Team Admin, Team Owner

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin, or the Instructor of the course, approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request nobody has approved or declined yet. Nobody can decide a Refund Request they raised themselves.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin or the course's Instructor has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and the learner loses access to the course.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin or the course's Instructor has turned down, with a reason the learner can see. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Undoing a decision**:
Whoever approved or declined a Refund Request can take it back within five minutes, and it becomes an Open Refund Request again as if it had never been decided. After that, the decision is final.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Reopening

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund whose Refund Request is approved, where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
